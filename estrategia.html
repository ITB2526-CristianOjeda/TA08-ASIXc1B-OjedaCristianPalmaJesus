Strategic Goals & Key Indicators

Context and methodology for achieving a 30% reduction in 3 years.

Measurable Objectives

Reduce idle electrical consumption in classrooms, extend hardware lifespan through circular economy, and reduce office materials by 30% compared to 2025.

Key Indicators

Monthly kWh billed for servers/classrooms, kg of WEEE (e-waste) managed internally, and total reams of paper consumed.

Implementation Context & Circular Economy

Energy Efficiency: Total Classroom Automation

Current Flaw Relying on users to turn off equipment is highly inefficient.

Tech Proposal Implementation of mandatory Group Policies (GPOs) and automated scripts (Cron, Wake-on-LAN) on the main server to forcefully shut down all equipment and monitors outside school hours.

WEEE Management & Second Life

Inspired by Google's circular economy practices, the hardware lifecycle procedure will be:

  • Prior Repair: Before discarding equipment to the Internal Recycling Point, hardware will be repaired or upgraded.
  • Upcycling: Adding SSDs or installing lightweight Linux distributions to extend useful life.
  • Critical Materials: Exclusive transport via an Authorized Waste Manager for obsolete equipment to prevent the loss of lithium and cobalt.

The financial and environmental impact of these improvements can be quantified in the Simulator dashboard.

Sustainable Measures Catalog

Click on any measure to view technical and implementation details.

Electricity

  • Solar Panels -40%
    Installation of photovoltaic panels on the roof for self-consumption, prioritizing school hours to absorb the expenditure peak of servers and classrooms.
  • LED + Sensors -15%
    Replacement of fluorescent tubes with LED panels. Integration of motion sensors in hallways and restrooms to prevent lights from staying on when not in use.
  • GPOs: Auto Shutdown -10%
    Configuration of policies (GPO) in Windows Server and scripts to force the automatic shutdown of all classroom PCs outside school hours.

Water

  • Faucet Sensors -15%
    Installation of infrared timers in restrooms to ensure water only flows when hands are detected, preventing improperly closed faucets.
  • Cistern Optimization -15%
    Dual-flush mechanisms in toilets and flow reducers (aerators) in faucets to minimize liters wasted per use.

Office

  • "Zero Paper" Culture -20%
    Campaign for students and teachers: avoid printing notes and prioritize 100% digital submissions through the Virtual Campus (Moodle/Classroom).
  • Digital Procedures -10%
    Replacement of physical enrollments, authorizations, and incident reports with web forms and automated digital signatures.

Cleaning

  • Service Optimization -20%
    Restructuring of cleaning rounds using efficient scrubbers and eliminating disposable paper towels in favor of low-consumption air dryers.
  • Eco-concentrated Chems. -10%
    Transition to biodegradable super-concentrated cleaners. Reduces plastics, carbon footprint in transport, and toxic spills.

3-Year ESG Roadmap

Year 1 (2025-2026)

Initial audit. Creation of Internal WEEE Recycling Point and partial digitalization of bureaucratic and administrative processes.

Progressive goal: 33% of the final target

Year 2 (2026-2027)

Deployment of efficient hardware. Implementation of scripts and GPOs on the main server for the automatic shutdown of all classrooms.

Progressive goal: 66% of the final target

Year 3 (2027-2028)

"Green IT" culture fully integrated. Complete optimization of supplies (solar panels, water sensors, eco chemicals).
