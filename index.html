📉 IT Action Plan: How to reduce impact by 30% in 3 years

🎯 Measurable Objectives

Reduce standby power consumption in classrooms, extend hardware lifespan (circular economy), and reduce office materials by 30% compared to 2025.

📊 Key Indicators

Monthly kWh billed for servers/classrooms, kg of WEEE managed, and paper packs.

🛠️ Concrete Actions ("Green IT" - Phase 2)

⚡ Energy Efficiency: Total Classroom Automation

Critique of current practice

Relying on users to turn off equipment is inefficient.

New Technological Proposal
  • Implementation of mandatory Group Policies (GPOs).
  • Use of automated scripts (Cron, Wake-on-LAN) on the main server.
  • Turn off all computers and monitors outside school hours to eliminate standby consumption.

🖥️ WEEE Management and Second Life (Circular Economy)

Inspired by the circular economy practices of Google's servers, the procedure will be:

  • Prior repair: Before discarding equipment to the Internal Recycling Point, attempts will be made to repair or upgrade the hardware.
  • Upgrade: Add SSD disks or install lightweight Linux distributions to extend their lifespan.

💧 Water, Paper & 🗑️ Maintenance

  • Consumables: Replacement of single-use containers with bulk systems and digitization of procedures.
  • Critical materials: For obsolete equipment, exclusive transport through an Authorized Waste Manager to avoid the loss of materials such as lithium or coltan.

🗓️ 3-Year Roadmap

  • Year 1 (Short-term):

    Creation of the Internal WEEE Recycling Point and partial digitization.

    Goal: 10% Reduction
  • Year 2 (Medium-term):

    Implementation and testing of Cron and GPO scripts on the main server to power off classrooms.

    Goal: 20% Reduction
  • Year 3 (Long-term):

    Fully integrated "Green IT" culture, massive PC repair with Linux/SSDs.

    Goal achieved: 30% Reduction
